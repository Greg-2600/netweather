US hubs

The same health treemap as /map, one level closer: each block is a US hub city, and each box inside it is one public airport or city-portal endpoint. Block and box area grows with severity × time in state; colour follows the same diverging health scale. Hover a box for the site identity, endpoint, raw signal, and why the classifier gave it that status. City and state come from the name convention “City, ST hub — site”, not a schema column.
